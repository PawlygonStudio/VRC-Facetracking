## [2.0.0-beta.2] - 2026-10-10
- New Pupil Emulation component in the `!Pawlygon - Face Tracking` prefab: on headsets without pupil tracking, the pupils drift gently and react to blinks while eye tracking is active, then hand over to real pupil tracking when the headset has it (per eye on avatars with left/right pupil blendshapes).
  - Mood reactions, on by default: wide eyes constrict the pupils, a frown on both sides dilates them.
  - Fake pupil layers already in the avatar's animators (generated by Pawlygon Unity Tools' Face Tracking Extras, or made by hand) are emptied in the uploaded copy and listed in the inspector.
- New Face Tracking Emulation component in the prefab: fills in shapes from related ones for trackers that don't send them, and limits shapes that fight each other. Emulations only add to the tracked value. Driven by native face tracking expressions, without animators or synced parameters.
  - On by default: tongue opens jaw, brows up with wide eyes, brows down with squint, and the 1.x limits (lip funnel relaxed by chin raise, mouth raisers relaxed by a closed mouth, tongue pulled in by the lip funnel).
  - Optional: angry brows, sad brows, and further limits (lips relaxed by the funnel, jaw and frown reduced by sideways mouth movement, lip suck relaxed by a closed mouth).
- Face Tracking Guards drives `EyeTrackingActive` and `LipTrackingActive` whenever an animator reads them (e.g. Face Tracking Extras or 1.x custom animations), even with Blinking off. Blinking now only decides whether blink layers get guards.
- Guards no longer treat this package's own animator layers as blink or gesture layers.
- Inspector notes wrap instead of running off the inspector.

## [2.0.0-beta.1] - 2026-10-10
Native face tracking for the VRChat Avatars SDK face tracking beta. Everything is applied at build time, without changing the avatar in the scene.
- New `!Pawlygon - Face Tracking` prefab with four components:
  - Face Tracking: sets up native face tracking on the avatar descriptor (meshes, blendshape mapping, visemes, eyelids, eye gaze).
  - Face Tracking Parameters: feeds FT/v2 and OSCm/Proxy parameters used by existing animators from native face tracking.
  - Face Tracking Guards: pauses gesture expressions and blink animations while face / eye tracking is active, with a Disable Gestures menu toggle.
  - Blendshape Fixes: drives anti-clipping fix shapes like MouthSmileLeft-EyeClosedLeft.
- Detects the 1.x OSC prefab and offers to remove it from the build.
- Build Test Copy in the Hierarchy context menu (!Pawlygon > Face Tracking).
- Fixed compiler warnings in the Threshold Editor and Clean OSC tools.
- Requires a VRChat Avatars SDK with native face tracking (3.11.0 beta or newer).
- The 1.x OSC prefabs, animators and animations moved to `Deprecated/` and are still included for avatars using VRCFaceTracking. Existing references keep working.
